# Nowak Workshop OS v7.9.7

## Open purchase-order accounting
- Sent and partially received purchase orders are treated as incoming stock.
- Suggested purchase orders now show quantities already on order.
- New purchase orders contain only the additional quantities still required.

## Combined supplier shipments
- Select two or more open purchase orders from the same supplier in Purchase Orders.
- Receive them as one shipment with one invoice, exchange rate, freight, tax, duty and payment-fee entry.
- Shared costs are allocated across all received items before weighted landed costs are saved.
- Each original purchase order keeps its own receiving history and status.
