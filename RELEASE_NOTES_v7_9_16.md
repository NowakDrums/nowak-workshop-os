# Nowak Workshop OS v7.9.16

## Tension-rod rules

- Tension-rod quantities now use drum type and the lug/claw count per hoop.
- 10, 12 and 13 inch toms use 6 rods per hoop (12 total).
- 14, 16 and 18 inch floor toms use 8 rods per hoop (16 total).
- 12 and 13 inch snares use 8 rods per hoop (16 total).
- 14 inch snares use 10 rods per hoop (20 total).
- 18 and 20 inch bass drums use 8 rods per side (16 total).
- 22 and 24 inch bass drums use 10 rods per side (20 total).
- Toms, floor toms and snares use 45 mm rods; bass drums use 110 mm rods.
- Chrome hardware uses Stainless Steel rods, Brass hardware uses Brass Plated rods, and Black Nickel hardware uses Black Nickel rods.
- The selected finish now carries through to inventory requirements, reorder planning and purchase-order colour/code output.

No Supabase migration is required.
