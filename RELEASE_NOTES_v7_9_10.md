# Nowak Workshop OS v7.9.10

- Construction filtering now normalises legacy values, spacing and capitalisation, so all Stave toms, floor toms and bass drums remain visible when Stave is selected.
- Repairs and modifications can be scheduled for Today, Tomorrow or a chosen date.
- Repair jobs store estimated workshop hours and contribute to the daily planned workload total.
- Scheduled repairs appear in the unified Workshop Today planner and can be opened directly from the plan.
- Repair planner entries are visually distinguished from drum-production tasks.

Run `supabase/v7_9_10_repair_planning.sql` once before scheduling repairs.
